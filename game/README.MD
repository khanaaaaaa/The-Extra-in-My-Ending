# The Extra In My Ending

## What It Is

A RENPY game inspired by 'Surviving Romance' manhwa. Always been fascinated by classic Isekai trope. So, the premise is similar to it but my twist is that there's not zombies.

## Why I Made It

I made it for Zoneout originally but got too busy to complete it. Also this project has my beautiful voice in the prelogue because I wanted to test it out. Worked quiet well and will probably work on voice acting on another larger scale project.

## How I Made It

Tech Stack:
- RenPy

Uhh, I've worked with RENPY a lot but aforementioned I never tried actually giving the characters a voice, so it was quite simple. But exporting from my tablet to my laptop and then converting the audio type was very time consuming.

## What I Learnt and What I Struggled With

Adding voices to my project is fun. Though it's cringe listening to it again and again but to be cringe is to be free anyways. Also I learnt that I just write dialogues without even defining the character and have a lot of spelling mistakes lol. I guess I'm a master at adding sounds now.
